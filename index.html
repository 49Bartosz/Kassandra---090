Hi, I'm Kassandra.

I'm the photographer behind KASS STUDIO, based in Den Haag. I believe a photo should feel like the moment did: the laugh you almost missed, the light on a quiet street, the people who make a day worth remembering.

I shoot people, places and memories, with a soft, natural and honest style. No stiff poses, just real moments that you'll want to look back on for years.

You can also find me on TikTok, where I share my work and behind-the-scenes.

Follow @framesbykass

What I photograph

People

Portraits, couples, friends and family. Relaxed sessions where you can just be yourself.

Places

Cityscapes, architecture and travel photography, from Den Haag's streets to places a little further away.

Memories

Birthdays, graduations, small events and celebrations, captured so you can enjoy them again later.

Content

Photos for your brand, socials or small business that feel personal and look professional.
